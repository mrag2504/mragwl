🚀 Wobbly Life Space Update is LIVE!

What’s up everyone! MrAG here.

The moment we’ve all been waiting for is finally here — the official Wobbly Life Space Update has landed!

This is one of the biggest updates the game has ever received. You can now leave the ground behind, jump into a rocket, and explore the vastness of space. New planets, floating asteroids, cool space stations, and a bunch of brand-new characters are waiting for you.

What’s New?

  • Full space exploration with rockets and spaceships
  • New characters including the Space Captain, Robot Engineer, and the mysterious SDA explorer
  • Floating asteroids, space stations, and alien-looking creatures

The update completely changes the way you play. One minute you’re running around the city doing wobbly chaos, and the next you’re flying through space with your friends. The new environments look amazing and the characters are as funny as ever.

I’ve already started making videos on this update — from first looks to funny moments and how to unlock the new space items. Make sure you subscribe so you don’t miss any of them!
